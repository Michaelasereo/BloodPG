# Private Closed Beta - BloodPG

## About BloodPG

BloodPG is a comprehensive health tracking application designed to help users monitor their blood pressure readings and manage medications. The platform enables users to:

- **Track Blood Pressure**: Log daily AM and PM readings with systolic and diastolic values
- **Manage Medications**: Record medications associated with each reading
- **View Records**: Access a comprehensive table of all entries with date filtering
- **Generate Reports**: Export PDF reports for healthcare providers
- **Secure Storage**: Data is securely stored with user authentication

## Importance of Private Closed Beta

This private closed beta is critical for ensuring BloodPG meets real-world user needs before public launch. Your participation will help us:

1. **Identify Usability Issues**: Discover any challenges users face when entering data and navigating the interface
2. **Validate Core Features**: Ensure blood pressure tracking, medication management, and reporting functions work as intended
3. **Test Edge Cases**: Find and fix issues with data validation, date handling, and form interactions
4. **Improve User Experience**: Gather feedback on the interface, workflow, and overall usability
5. **Ensure Data Integrity**: Verify that data is saved correctly, dates update properly, and tables populate accurately

Your feedback during this 5-day testing period (December 2-6, 2024) will directly shape the final product and help us create a reliable, user-friendly health tracking tool.

## Testing Requirements

- **Duration**: 5 consecutive days (December 2-6, 2024)
- **Device**: Must be completed on PC/Laptop (not mobile devices)
- **Commitment**: Daily participation is required to test date progression and data continuity
- **Focus**: Follow your assigned test scenario carefully and report any issues or unexpected behavior

Thank you for participating in this important phase of BloodPG's development!
