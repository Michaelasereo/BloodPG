# Beta Test Instructions - Group 4
**Testing Period: December 2-6, 2024 (5 consecutive days)**

## Overview
You will sign in immediately and enter values for 5 consecutive days, using normal blood pressure values and the same medications each day.

---

## Day 1 - Monday, December 2, 2024

1. Open the BloodPG application
2. **Sign in immediately** with your Google account
3. Verify the date shows as **December 2, 2024**
4. Enter blood pressure values (use **normal values**):
   - **AM Reading**:
     - AM Systolic: 125
     - AM Diastolic: 83
   - **PM Reading**:
     - PM Systolic: 122
     - PM Diastolic: 81
5. Add medications:
   - Enter **2 medications**
   - Use the **same medications** you'll use for all 5 days
   - Example: "Metoprolol 25mg" and "Atorvastatin 20mg"
6. Save the entry
7. Verify the table populates with your entry
