# Beta Test Instructions - Group 1
**Testing Period: December 2-6, 2024 (5 consecutive days)**

## Overview
You will test the app by entering values without signing in first, then signing in, and verifying that data persists and displays correctly.

---

## Day 1 - Monday, December 2, 2024

### Step 1: Test Without Signing In
1. Open the BloodPG application
2. **DO NOT sign in yet**
3. Enter blood pressure values:
   - **AM Reading**: Try entering more than 3 digits (e.g., 1234/567)
   - **AM Reading**: Try entering decimal numbers (e.g., 120.5/80.3)
   - Note what happens - does the app accept or reject these values?
4. Enter **only PM (night) reading**, leave AM (day) blank:
   - PM Systolic: 130
   - PM Diastolic: 85
5. Add medications:
   - Enter a **different medication** than you'll use on other days
   - Try entering a **very long dosage value** (e.g., "Take 2 tablets twice daily with food and water")
   - If it's invalid, enter a correct value
6. Save the entry
7. Check if the table populates with your entry

### Step 2: Sign In
1. Click the sign-in button
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Verify that your previously entered data is still visible
4. Check if the date shows as December 2, 2024
